Builds on assessments/playtest5/plans/1066-11-11.txt. Strategic posture is
stable (no wars, no factions, all 13 vassals neutral) — this plan set is

================================================================

Confirmed via the new gs.claims_held_by() helper (reads alive_data.claim
directly off the character, rather than scanning every title's claim list):

  c_zatec    — claim, NOT pressed. Zatec is already held by Vratislav's own
               vassal Časlav Vrsovid — this claim is inert/inherited, no
               action implied.
  d_meissen  — claim, PRESSED.
  d_lausitz  — claim, PRESSED.

"Pressed" means Vratislav has already formally pressed both claims —
they're primed to use, not just sitting dormant.

On provenance: there is no recoverable record of *how* these claims were
acquired. Checked title history (landed_titles), character_memory_manager
(12,110 memory entries, 61 types — none of them claim-grant-related), and
title_and_vassal_change_manager (empty, just a counter). CK3 does not log
"liege granted you a claim" as a persistent event anywhere in the save —
it's presumably a liege/council interaction effect that leaves no trail
beyond the resulting claim entry itself. Flagging this clearly rather than
guessing: the player's own account ("granted by my liege") is the only
record we have, and that's fine.

I. SHORT-TERM — Meissen
================================================================

[CONTEXT] Timing constraint: Vratislav is currently in Vaticano (Rome)
  attending Heinrich's coronation (intent: bear witness — passive
  attendance, no scheme). His travel plan has him returning to Praha with
  an ETA of 1068.9.11 — about 5 months out. Otto, Duke of Meissen, is
  *also* at the same coronation right now, equally away from his own
  capital. Functionally: neither side is positioned to fight immediately;
  realistic war timing is after Vratislav is home, not before.

[CAMPAIGN ASSESSMENT]
  Target: Otto, Duke of Meissen (id 31798), age 41. Calm, deceitful, brave.
    Intrigue-focused (tier 1, "Skulduggery" perks: Truth Is Relative,
    Digging For Dirt, Kidnapper, Court Of Shadows, Prepared For Anything,
    Twice Schemed). Personal prowess strong (eff. 11) but his investment is
    in scheming, not field command — the realistic risk from Otto is a
    counter-scheme (assassination/kidnap attempt) during the war, not a
    battlefield surprise.
  Power comparison: Otto — 1,730 levy, 43,140 power. Vratislav — 3,981
    troops, 106,020 power. We hold roughly 2.45x his power before any ally
    intervenes — a clearly favorable matchup.
  Otto's ally: Bertrand, Duke of Provence (852 levy, 25,940 power).
    Geographically distant (southern France) — even fully honored, the
    combined opposing force (43,140 + 25,940 = 69,080) stays well under
    our 106,020. Low risk either way; the alliance is real but not
    decisive at this size advantage.
  Casus belli: the pressed claim on d_meissen is ready to use — no
    additional diplomatic prep needed to unlock the war itself.
  Imperial risk: Heinrich's crown authority is at the lowest tier
    (crown_authority_0) and he is currently uncrowned (the coronation
    Vratislav is attending resolves that). At CA0, intra-empire vassal wars
    are essentially unconstrained — low risk of imperial interference for
    a vassal-vs-vassal conquest war.
  Mechanical correction (player caught this): the claim is on the DUCHY
    title (d_meissen) itself, not on any of its counties. Winning the war
    transfers d_meissen — the Margravate — to Vratislav; Otto, Benno, and
    Eberhard keep their county-tier holdings exactly as they are now. Otto
    is demoted from independent Duke to Count of Meissen (+ Dresden) and
    becomes Vratislav's vassal, same as Benno and Eberhard. This is much
    cleaner than a land-grab: no domain_limit issue at all, since no new
    counties enter Vratislav's personal domain.
  Scope clarification (resolves the earlier open question): Dedo's
    Osterland/Cottbus and Friedrich's Görlitz are NOT part of this war's
    footprint. A character can only have one liege at a time — Dedo's
    liege is Heinrich (he holds those counties as his own personal domain,
    as independent Duke of Lausitz), not Otto. They're de jure Meissen but
    de facto Lausitz, and a Conquest CB can't seize land from a party not
    in the war. The real Meissen footprint is just Otto + Benno + Eberhard.

[KINGDOM OF BOHEMIA — sequencing, corrected]
  Two independent requirements to create a kingdom-tier title (per CK3's
  actual rule, confirmed via wiki after an earlier back-and-forth on this):
    1. Control ≥51% of the title's de jure counties (legitimacy lowers this).
    2. Hold either two titles of lower rank (two duchies), or one title of
       equal/higher rank (already holding a kingdom/empire title).
  Checked #1 directly against the save: Vratislav already controls 14/14
    (100%) of Kingdom of Bohemia's de jure counties (own domain: Praha,
    Litoměřice, Plzeň, Doudleby, Boleslav, Hradec; vassals: Žatec/Časlav,
    Olomouc+Přerov/Ota, Opava/Jan, Brno+Jihlava+Znojmo/Konrád). Comfortably
    clears #1 on its own, no Meissen needed for the percentage.
  #2 is the actual gate: Vratislav currently holds exactly one duchy
    (d_bohemia). Meissen would be the second — this is what conquering it
    actually unlocks, not new land. Confirms the player's original plan.
  Sequencing: win Meissen → hold 2 duchies → create Kingdom of Bohemia →
    grant Meissen back out (see below). Title creation appears to be a
    one-time eligibility check, not an ongoing requirement, so there's no
    obstacle to granting Meissen away immediately after using it to qualify.
  Strategic upside worth weighing in: King-tier status unlocks peer-king
    alliances (Hungary, Poland, Denmark all appeared in the nearby-rulers
    list) — this is the most direct fix available for the standing "zero
    alliances" problem the Marshal/Chancellor briefs keep flagging, and
    that alliance gap is also the hard prerequisite for the Lausitz war
    (see below). Claiming the Crown isn't just a status upgrade — it's on
    the critical path for Lausitz too.

[WHO TO PROMOTE — granting Meissen back out]
  Once King, Meissen can be granted to a vassal so they (not Vratislav)
  deal directly with day-to-day Saxon-bloc management — the player's
  proposed move. Three real candidates, since Osterland/Cottbus/Görlitz
  aren't part of this war's footprint (see above):
    — Otto himself, reinstated as Duke under Vratislav instead of
      independent. Zero local disruption — Benno and Eberhard already
      answer to him, nothing changes for them. Risk: Otto is a dedicated
      schemer (Intrigue/Skulduggery — Court Of Shadows, Kidnapper, Digging
      For Dirt, Twice Schemed) who'd be taking this demotion personally. A
      capable, humiliated schemer as your own duke needs active management
      — a hook, a hostage (a fostered child, mirroring the Judita/Siegfried
      arrangement in reverse), or a marriage tie, not just trust.
    — Benno (id 30715), elevated from count to duke. Trusting + just, weak
      personal military, least likely of the three to ever scheme against
      Vratislav, and a big promotion like this likely buys real gratitude.
      Open question: can he actually hold authority over Otto, now his
      nominal subordinate despite Otto's stronger personality and prior
      seniority? Scholar build, not a natural enforcer.
    — A trusted Bohemian/Přemyslid figure instead of any local. Maximum
      loyalty assurance, but doesn't remove Otto from the picture (he's
      still there, just under a different, more culturally distant
      overlord), and pulls someone away from Bohemia's own core vassal
      network.
  Lean: Benno, on personality fit, but this is a judgment call the player
    should make directly — it hinges on a read of Otto/Benno dynamics that
    isn't fully visible from the save data.
  Bigger-picture integration note: this would be Bohemia's first Saxon/
    Franconian-culture vassal bloc. Same faith (Catholic), so no religious
    friction, but a culture gap exists either way. Judita's pending
    betrothal to Siegfried von Nordheim (Bavaria) already starts a German-
    marriage pattern; extending that logic to whichever Meissen vassals end
    up in Vratislav's orbit is worth a deliberate pass.

II. LONG-TERM — Lausitz
================================================================

[CAMPAIGN CONTEXT]
  Target: Dedo, Duke of Lausitz (id 29180), age 58. Cynical, vengeful,
    wrathful — three vice traits, Dread 14.7, "aggressive_attacker"
    commander trait, perk investment entirely in authority/war (Serve The
    Crown, Hard Rule, Absolute Control, Bellum Justum, Engineered For
    Destruction). Effective Martial 16 — a genuinely dangerous personal
    commander, not just a strong realm. He currently also serves as
    Heinrich's Imperial Spymaster — standing access to the Emperor that
    Otto doesn't have, and worth weighing if this war ever needs imperial
    goodwill on our side.
  Power comparison: Dedo — 2,605 levy, 72,120 power, vs. our 106,020 — a
    ~1.47x edge for us alone, noticeably thinner than the Meissen matchup.
  Dedo's ally: Ernst, Duke of Austria (d_osterreich) — 2,176 levy, 53,860
    power. This one is not a paper alliance: combined Lausitz+Austria
    (72,120 + 53,860 = 125,980) would outweigh us (106,020). This is the
    determining factor for why Lausitz is the long-term project, not the
    short-term one — either our own power needs to grow past that combined
    threshold, or the Austria alliance needs to be neutralized/divorced
    first, or we need an ally of our own to offset it.
  Connective tissue with Meissen: Dedo's vassal Friedrich already holds
    Görlitz, de jure part of Meissen, and Dedo personally holds Osterland
    and Cottbus inside de jure Meissen as Otto's vassal. A Meissen war
    likely touches Dedo's interests indirectly before we ever declare on
    him directly — worth watching how he reacts to the Meissen war as an
    early read on his posture, and worth noting his current activity at
    the coronation: his stated intent there is "Disrupt Loyalists" — he is
    actively scheming against Heinrich's loyalists right now, consistent
    with his profile. Worth finding out (next save) whether that scheme
    succeeded and what it cost him.

[STANDING WORK]
  Establish at least one alliance before committing to Lausitz — this is
    already the Marshal/Chancellor's #1 generic priority (isolation), and
    it's a hard prerequisite here specifically: an ally large enough to
    offset or deter Austria's 53,860 power changes this from "risky" to
    "favorable." Re-evaluate Lausitz seriously only after that's in place,
    or after our own power has grown enough that 125,980 is no longer a
    threatening number.

IV. TOOLING (built this session)
================================================================

[TOOL] Claims — GameState.claims_held_by(char_id) (ck3/gamestate.py)
  Reads alive_data.claim directly (list of {title, pressed?}) rather than
  scanning every title's claim list. Returns title_id/title_key/title/
  pressed for each. No provenance data exists in the save (see section 0).

[TOOL] Carmen Sandiego — ck3/evaluators/carmen_sandiego.py +
  run_carmen_sandiego.py
  "Where in the world is...?" — resolves a character's current location,
  imprisonment status, activity (hosting/attending, with intent and host),
  and travel plan (destination + ETA), pulling from alive_data.location,
  alive_data.involved_activity_data, gs.activities, and gs.travel_plans
  (the latter two newly wired into GameState.__init__).
  CLI: python run_carmen_sandiego.py --char <id> [<id> ...]
  This is how the Rome/coronation picture above was put together — confirms
  Vratislav, Otto, and Dedo are all at the same event right now, and
  surfaced Dedo's scheme intent ("Disrupt Loyalists") as a side effect of
  just checking his location.
