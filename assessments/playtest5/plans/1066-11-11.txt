III. TOOLING (built this session)
================================================================

[TOOL] Matchmaker — ck3/evaluators/matchmaker.py + run_matchmaker.py
  Codifies the manual betrothal screen above into a reusable query:
  find_candidates(gs, char_id, age_window=, faith_mode=, track=, max_zone=,
  exclude_vices=, require_virtue=, limit=) → MatchmakerReport.
  - Splits candidates into noble (alliance-relevant, has dynasty house) vs
    lowborn (matrilineal-relevant) tracks.
  - Classifies CK3 personality traits into virtue/vice sets (sourced from
    the actual trait pairs in game/common/traits/00_traits.txt, not from
    save data — CK3 doesn't tag traits as virtue/vice itself).
  - Resolves faith/culture via dynasty-house-head fallback for unlanded
    characters (same convention as character_profile()).
  - Resolves geographic proximity via the employer chain for unlanded
    courtiers, reusing geography.py's zone_between().
  CLI: python run_matchmaker.py --char <id> [--age-window N]
    [--track noble|lowborn] [--max-zone 0-3] [--faith same_faith|
    same_religion|any] [--allow-vices] [--require-virtue]
  Use this directly against the next save to re-screen Judita (and start
  screening Ludmila once she's closer to marriageable age).
