[BUG FOUND + FIXED] Otakar and Ordulf were showing up in the relationships
  report as "own realm" rivals — which would mean they're Vratislav's own
  vassals. They're not; both are fully independent/foreign dukes. Root
  cause: RelationshipsEvaluator._char_location() (ck3/evaluators/
  relationships.py) treated "holds any title" as equivalent to "is in the
  player's realm," with no actual liege-chain check. Fixed: now walks the
  real vassal-contract chain back to the player; added a proper
  "foreign_ruler" category distinct from "own_realm". Verified against
  this save and the 1070.11.5 save with no regressions.

================================================================
1. WAR-RETARGETING — new mechanical knowledge, documented
================================================================

Player's explanation, confirmed and now written into the codebase: Dedo
was independently fighting Otto for the Margravate of Meissen — the same
title Vratislav was also pursuing. When Vratislav's own war concluded and
he took the title, Dedo's still-active war didn't resolve — it retargeted,
with Vratislav inheriting the defender role. Result: "I just won my war for
Meissen" became "I am now being attacked over Meissen" in a single
transition, with no warning beyond a new entry in active_wars.

Documented in ck3/evaluators/military.py (module docstring + ActiveWar
dataclass comment) so this isn't re-discovered the hard way next time.
Noted in ideas.txt as a candidate for an actual Marshal-brief feature later
(proactively flag "this looks like an inherited war") — not built yet,
needs the title-history-as-war-log infrastructure first.

Strategic implication going forward: winning a contested title doesn't
necessarily end the contest. Worth checking, before declaring on Lausitz,
whether any third party is independently pressing a claim on d_lausitz
right now — winning that war could hand Vratislav a second, unplanned
war the same way Meissen did.
