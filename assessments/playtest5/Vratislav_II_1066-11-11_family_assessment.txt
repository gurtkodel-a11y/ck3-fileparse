============================================================
LONG-TERM / MARRIAGE NOTES
============================================================
Liege is Heinrich, Emperor of the Holy Roman Empire — Bohemia sits inside
the Empire's orbit, so HRE-internal marriages (or marriages that buy claims
within imperial vassal houses) carry the most direct strategic value for
consolidating Vratislav's position relative to his liege and rival vassals.
Jaromír (unlanded rival, in another court) reads as the recurring
pretender/rival figure from this dynasty's prior runs — a marriage or
alliance that isolates Jaromír's potential support base is worth keeping in
mind as the children come of age, rather than treating these as purely
personality-driven matches.

No alliances currently held (0), which is unusual for a duke this early —
matrilineal or regular marriages for Judita/Ludmila that create alliances
with neighboring counts/dukes would directly address that gap once they're
of marriageable age.
