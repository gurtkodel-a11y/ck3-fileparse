Assessment — Vratislav II Přemyslid, King of Bohemia
Date: 1077.2.10  |  Playtest 2
================================================================

In the six years since 1071, Bohemia underwent a quiet but fundamental
transformation. No major wars are visible in the title history. Instead,
the tools see a burst of administrative and diplomatic action in the opening
months of 1072 that changed the character of the realm permanently. What
began as a shaky duchy with six restive Saxon vassals and no alliances is
now a kingdom with three royal alliances and a court positioned at the center
of Central European Catholic politics.

The cost of that ascent, and the structural risks underneath it, are what this
assessment is about.


================================================================
I. WHAT HAPPENED (1071.2.5 → 1077.2.10)
================================================================

CONFIRMED FROM TITLE HISTORY:

  1072.1.12 — k_bohemia created for Vratislav
    The Kingdom of Bohemia was either claimed directly (gold + prestige path)
    or granted through imperial favor. Given the speed — only 11 months after
    we identified this as the long-term savings goal — the title-creation
    mechanic seems most likely. Either way, Vratislav went from Duke to King
    in the first quarter of 1072. Remarkable pace.

  1072.1.26 — d_meissen granted to Reginar from Vratislav
    Executed as planned, within two weeks of the royal elevation. The duchy
    grant to Reginar was the first act of the new kingdom's administration.
    His current opinion is +55 (received_title_duchy +60, partially offset
    by a minority conversion modifier). The plan worked.

  1072.3.17 — d_thuringia CREATED for Ludwig Ludowinger
    Ludwig's elevation to Duke of Thuringia (title created, not transferred
    from Vratislav's holdings) followed the Meissen grant by seven weeks.
    Three saxon dukes in the realm now: Ludwig (Thuringia), Reginar (Meissen),
    and Jan (Count of Opava, theocracy). This resolved the Saxon stabilization
    question through elevation rather than absorption.

CONFIRMED FROM RELATIONSHIPS AND PERSONAL RELATIONS:

  Alliances formed:
    — Ludmila married Thorgils (Danish; currently in Sjælland) → Danish
      alliance with King Svend Estrid
    — Břetislav paired with Agathe (search inconclusive, likely Norman) →
      English alliance with William III de Normandie, King of England
    — Polish alliance (via Šwiétosława) confirmed active

  Three royal alliances. At the start of 1071, we had none. At 1077, we have
  the Kings of England, Denmark, and Poland bound to Bohemia by marriage bonds.
  This is the most significant development in the playtest.

  Judita died 1075.11.18.
    She was 19. She was in Regensburg when we last assessed her (possible
    betrothal), but no marriage record survives and no betrothal is visible
    in the save. The planned Styrian/Austrian marriage was never confirmed.
    She is now listed as deceased in the family report, between the primary
    spouse entry and Břetislav. The mystery of Regensburg remains unresolved.
    We do not know if she died before a marriage was arranged or after.

INFERRED (not confirmed from data):

  The mechanism for k_bohemia is uncertain — title creation vs. imperial
  grant. The speed suggests gold/prestige creation rather than waiting for
  Heinrich's goodwill (we had -20 disapproving opinion of him and no plan to
  fix it). But the fact that Ludwig received a duchy the same month suggests
  this may also have been part of an imperial deal ("I'll grant you the
  kingdom if you elevate my Saxon vassals"). We cannot confirm from the save.

  How Vladislav ended up at the Polish court (guardian = Bolesław III Piast)
  is unknown. It is a significant diplomatic placement — the King of Poland
  personally educating a Přemyslid son suggests a deliberate arrangement,
  possibly tied to the Polish alliance. Whether this was a negotiated quid pro
  quo or an organic event is opaque to us.

III. REGINAR'S WAR — THE MEISSEN SUCCESSION STRUGGLE
================================================================

[NOTE: This war was initially missed in this assessment. Corrected on review.]

Active war (ID 50331738) started 1073.7.25. In July 1073 — just six months
after Reginar received the Duchy of Meissen — Benno von Lóningen, Count of
Wolkenstein (one of Reginar's own Saxon counts), formed a claimant faction
and declared war to seat Otto von Weimar as Duke of Meissen.

THE PARTIES:

  Attacker: Benno von Lóningen, Count of Wolkenstein (age 57)
    — Faction leader. One of Reginar's vassal counts; this is a rebellion
      from within the duchy. Also one of the Saxon counts the 1071 plans
      identified as a marriage alliance candidate.
    Current strength: 392 / 520 max

  Claimant: Otto von Weimar, Count of Meissen (age 50)
    — The man who would become duke if the faction wins. Also the same
      Otto who had -25 opinion of Vratislav from the original 1066 war.
    Current strength: 191 / 191 max

  Defender: Reginar, Duke of Meissen (age 47)
    Current strength: 809 / 875 max

  Called to war (defender): Ludwig Ludowinger, Duke of Thuringia (age 62)
    — Reginar's neighbor and fellow Saxon vassal called in to help defend.
    Current strength: 1798 / 1885 max

THE CURRENT STATE:

  Warscore: +4.846 (attacker-favorable — meaning Otto's faction is winning)
  Duration: ongoing since July 1073 (3.5 years at this save date)

  One battle on record: in 1073, Benno attacked with 1,847 troops (likely
  the full faction mustered at war's start) against Reginar's 526. Benno
  won and took province 3033 (within Meissen territory). Warscore: +4.846.

  Since that battle, no further engagements — and the troop balance has
  shifted dramatically:
    Defender side (Reginar + Ludwig): ~2,607 current / ~2,760 max
    Attacker side (Benno + Otto):       ~583 current /   ~711 max

  Reginar now outnumbers the faction roughly 4-to-1. The war is almost
  certainly winnable. But Reginar hasn't pressed the advantage — the
  warscore has sat at +4.846 for however long since that opening battle,
  meaning no sieges or battles have gone Reginar's way.

WHY THIS MATTERS:

  If Benno's faction wins, Otto becomes Duke of Meissen. That would:
    — Replace Reginar (+55 opinion) with Otto (declared_war -25) as the
      Saxon duke controlling Meissen's ~875 max troops
    — Give Benno's faction a reward (likely county grants or influence),
      entrenching an anti-Reginar power structure in the duchy
    — Destabilize the core of the Saxon integration project — the entire
      rationale for granting d_meissen was to lock in Reginar's loyalty

  If Reginar wins (currently the military favorite), the internal Saxon
  dynamics still need watching: Benno remains a vassal in the duchy, and
  his willingness to rebel once suggests it could happen again.

WHAT VRATISLAV CAN DO:

  As Reginar's liege, Vratislav can call his own levies into the war on
  Reginar's side. With 4,205 max troops of his own, his entry would be
  decisive and immediate. This also sends a political signal: the King of
  Bohemia protects loyal dukes.

  Ludwig is already in — which means Bohemia's two Saxon dukes are defending
  together, a heartening sign for the faction model. But the war's 3.5-year
  stalemate suggests Reginar has not been given (or used) the tools to push
  warscore into negative territory.

  Even without direct intervention, Vratislav can watch the situation and
  intervene only if Reginar's warscore deteriorates further.

IV. VRATISLAV: THE HEALTH PROBLEM
================================================================

Age 45 | Health 3.5 (below average) | Consumption trait

The "consumption" trait is a CK3 health condition (tuberculosis / wasting).
Health 3.5 is a meaningful step below the baseline. At 45 with a chronic
illness, the mortality risk over the next 5-10 years is real.

Vratislav's build has moved decisively into Stewardship:
  Education: Stewardship tier 5 (highest possible)
  Effective Stewardship: 23 (!!!)
  Lifestyle traits: architect, avaricious
  19 perks deep into the Stewardship tree

Skills: base Dipl 5, Mart 12, Stew 5, Intr 4, Lear 5, Prow 12
        eff.  Dipl 4, Mart 15, Stew 23, Intr -2, Lear 12, Prow 10

The Stewardship 23 is extraordinary for a ruler, and explains the realm's
economic stability and the gold available for the royal elevation in 1072.
The architect + avaricious lifestyle combination emphasizes domain building.
The Intrigue effective score of -2 is a vulnerability.

Dread 10.0 (up sharply from 0.5 in 1071). Enough to intimidate some vassals
but not dominant. The source of this dread increase is unknown.

V. THE SUCCESSION SITUATION
================================================================

[NOTE: The original draft of this assessment treated Konrád inheriting as
a crisis. Corrected on review: Konrád as player heir is acceptable. The
specific problem is Jaromír — second in line, excommunicated.]

The heir to the Kingdom of Bohemia is NOT Břetislav. It is Konrád Přemyslid.

Succession line for k_bohemia: [33299, 34156, 35734, 38159, ...]

  33299 = Konrád Přemyslid (Count of Brno, age 42, our Spymaster)
  34156 = Jaromír (age 38, EXCOMMUNICATED, our rival)
  35734 = unknown (likely Ota Přemyslid, Count of Olomouc, age 32)
  38159 = Břetislav (age 19)

This is Seniority succession — the eldest living male Přemyslid inherits,
regardless of parentage.

KONRÁD AS HEIR — WORKABLE:

  Konrád is 42, below-average health (3.9), loyal (+55 opinion), and our
  Spymaster. Playing as Konrád is a viable continuation — he is not hostile.
  If Vratislav dies before the succession law changes, Konrád inheriting is
  not ideal (he is not the character we've been developing) but it is not
  a disaster. He would have the same Saxon alliances to work with, the same
  kingdom, and a loyal base.

  Konrád's own heir is id 16787023 (Tas, his son), so the Přemyslid seniority
  line extends through him. This matters if Konrád inherits and then dies.

THE ACTUAL CRISIS: JAROMÍR IS SECOND:

  Jaromír (id 34156) is second in line, and he is excommunicated. If Konrád
  dies before the succession law changes, an excommunicated man becomes King
  of Bohemia. That is the specific catastrophe to prevent.

  An excommunicated king means:
    — Papal hostility (Anselmo is already -30; he would be incandescent
      about Jaromír on the throne)
    — Potential papal interdict on the realm
    — Likely faction pressure from Catholic vassals
    — Alliance viability at risk (England, Denmark are Catholic kingdoms)

  Jaromír's profile (arbitrary, lazy, wrathful, grudge against Jan) makes
  him personally unsuited to rule as well. The combination of political
  disqualification and character flaws is nearly total.

THE REAL PRIORITY:

  The goal is not "ensure Břetislav inherits" — it is "ensure Jaromír does
  not inherit." That is a narrower problem with more solutions:

  Option A: Change the succession law to Primogeniture
    — Removes Konrád and Jaromír from active succession; Břetislav inherits
    — Hardest to execute but the most complete fix

  Option B: Remove Jaromír from the succession line specifically
    — Excommunication alone doesn't remove him from Seniority succession
    — He needs to: enter the church/monastery, be disinherited, die, or be
      imprisoned long-term
    — His current "Develop County" task under Jan suggests he is already
      marginalized; pressing him into a monastery would resolve it cleanly

  Option C: Outlast Jaromír passively
    — Both Konrád (age 42, health 3.9) and Jaromír (age 38, health 3.9)
      have below-average health; natural mortality is not impossible
    — Ota Přemyslid (third in line, age 32, health 4.5 normal) is a healthier
      but longer-lived stepping stone if Konrád dies first

  If Konrád dies and the law has not changed, active intervention against
  Jaromír's succession becomes urgent. This is why it's worth monitoring Ota
  (third in line at 32 with normal health — a healthy Ota buying more time
  before Jaromír is a viable if uncomfortable scenario).
