What this assessment knows: everything visible in evaluator output and character

I. WHAT HAPPENED (INFERRED FROM DATA)
--------------------------------------

The gap between 1066.10.5 and 1071.2.5 cannot be narrated with confidence from
the save alone, but the opinion modifiers and vassal roster are unusually readable.

[Wars] The evidence:
  — Otto (von Weimar, likely Count of Meissen): -25 (declared_war)
  — Oda (id 37772, Otto's ward/daughter): -15 (declared_war_on_son_daughter)
  — Eberhard (Count of Naumburg): +20 (released_from_prison)
  — Ludwig (Ludowinger, Count of Schmalkalden): +20 (released_from_prison)
  — Tamar: -30 (imprisoned_me) — identity unknown, still holding a grudge

At least one war was fought, probably more. Eberhard and Ludwig were captured
and then released — a magnanimous act that converted enemies into qualified vassals.
Otto was declared against; Oda (almost certainly his daughter, now in Meissen)
carries the resentment. Tamar was imprisoned and has not forgiven it; Tamar
doesn't appear in the vassal list, so is either still imprisoned or at another
court — identity unclear without better lookup tools.

[Vassal expansion] Six new count-tier vassals appear who were not present at
game start: Benno von Lóningen (Wolkenstein), Ludwig Ludowinger (Schmalkalden),
Otto von Weimar (Meissen), Reginar (Dresden), Eberhard (Naumburg), Filibert
(Pula). This is a major expansion — from 4 count-tier feudal vassals to 10.
The geography is striking: Meissen, Dresden, Schmalkalden, Naumburg, Wolkenstein
are all in the Saxony/Thuringia corridor. How Bohemia absorbed this territory
is not fully clear from the data. The most likely mechanism in CK3: the Saxon
Wars, which were a major HRE internal conflict in this period; Vratislav sided
with Emperor Heinrich and was rewarded with Saxon counts as vassals after their
side won. However, the Emperor's current -20 disapproving opinion of Vratislav
(from Vratislav's direction, not Heinrich's) complicates this narrative —
something has soured the relationship.

What we would need to know this for certain: an event log or war history query
tool. The save has enough fingerprints to reconstruct the broad story but not
the sequence or exact mechanics.

[Judita in Regensburg] Judita is not in Praha. She is in Regensburg — a major
Bavarian city and imperial seat. This strongly suggests she has been betrothed
or sent to a German court. No guardian is listed for her, which may mean the
Jan of Opava guardianship plan was not executed (or was refused), or that she
left the realm before it could be arranged. Whether this is a betrothal to a
Bavarian nobleman or something else cannot be determined from the save alone.

[New daughter] KristYna (id 16800260) was born 1068.7.31. She is 2 and currently
ill. Šwiétosława's fourth child; this was not part of the 1066 planning horizon.

[Časlav Vrsovid fired from council] The Count of Žatec carries a -20
fired_from_council_opinion. He was in the candidate pool for Judita's guardian
(Diplomacy tier 4, recommended as fallback if Jan refused). The firing may have
been provoked or may simply reflect a political reshuffling after the wars.

[No alliances formed] Despite planning to secure Hungary first, Vratislav enters
1071 with zero formal alliances. This is the most significant strategic gap
between plan and reality.


II. MILITARY SITUATION
-----------------------

Realm ceiling: 4,438 max (3,612 levy + 826 MaA), up from ~3,287 at game start.
The increase comes primarily from the new Saxon vassals adding levy.

MaA regiments: unchanged from 1066 (zbrojnosh, light_horsemen, pikemen,
mangonel). No investment in new regiment types or expansion of existing ones.

Knights (6): Benno (9), Reginar (10), Záviš (10), Boreš (9), Vznata (9), Otto (9).
Notable: Reginar is now listed as both a knight AND the Count of Dresden (a vassal).
Benno von Lóningen similarly is a knight AND the Count of Wolkenstein. Two of
Bohemia's six knights are also county-tier vassals — an unusual overlap that the
report doesn't currently flag but would be worth tracking.

No active wars. The pagan belt to the north (Pomeranian Slavs, Baltic pagans,
Finnish pagans) remains intact and unengaged.

Power context: Bohemia at ~4,438 max troops sits in a plausible position relative
to the newly acquired Saxon vassals (combined ~4,000). The realm is substantially
larger than in 1066 but the player's personal domain is unchanged — still 7 holdings.

IV. STRATEGIC POSITION
-----------------------

[Realm size] Substantially larger than 1066 due to the Saxon vassal additions.
Total vassal levy has grown significantly. The new feudal counts (Meissen, Dresden,
Naumburg, Schmalkalden, Wolkenstein) are German, Catholic, and culturally distant.
Their loyalty is conditional: several carry active negative opinions (Otto -25,
Eberhard and Ludwig just barely +20 thanks to the magnanimous releases).

[Alliances] None. This was the single clearest planning failure of the 1066–1071
period. Hungary (Salamon Árpád, 3,166 troops) was the stated first-priority alliance
target. Poland (Bolesław III Piast, 2,322 troops — a succession occurred; the III
indicates generational change) is the West Slavic neighbor via Šwiétosława.
No formal external partner exists.

[Papal opinion] Unchanged at -30 impious. Pope Anselmo has not been placated.
This is a compounding risk: the Investiture conflict is a live thread in the
1070s HRE, and Bohemia enters that period already in Rome's disfavor.

[Heinrich] Vratislav's opinion of Heinrich is -20 (disapproving_opinion). Something
the Emperor did generated disapproval. The relationship with the paramount overlord
is not hostile, but it is not warm.

[Personal domain] Still 7 holdings; no development investment shown (all remain at 8
except Praha at 12). Čáslav was the stated priority investment — still unactioned.

  — Jaromír watch. He's embedded, not dormant. Track whether his Learning focus

[ONGOING CONCERNS]

    German counts is a plausible near-term risk.
  — Papal relations. Jan of Opava is still the most natural conduit. What has
    Jan actually done in these four years? He is still in court, still Fratislav's
    friend — but his effectiveness as a diplomatic bridge to Rome is unclear.


VI. WHAT BETTER TOOLS WOULD TELL US
-------------------------------------

These are genuine gaps in our picture, noted to inform future tooling priorities:

  — War history / event log: The single most valuable missing query. The vassal
    expansion, the opinion modifiers, and the Meissen/Saxony situation all scream
    "something significant happened" but the exact sequence is opaque. A tool
    that surfaces recent wars (active_wars closed since last save?) or major events
    would transform the 1066→1071 transition from archaeology into history.

  — Betrothal status: Judita is in Regensburg. Are she betrothed? To whom?
    The character profile doesn't surface this. A betrothal query tool would
    answer the single biggest open question about her trajectory.

  — Guardian finder: When writing the 1066 plans, we surveyed Konrád and Vladimír
    as martial guardians for Ludmila and didn't identify Ota Přemyslid (who turned
    out to be the obvious right answer — Martial tier 3, excellent personality).
    A "find best guardian for X focus within this realm" query would have surfaced
    him immediately.

  — Tamar identity: Someone named Tamar was imprisoned by Vratislav and carries
    -30 opinion. Not in the vassal list. Unclear whether still imprisoned or at
    another court. A named-character lookup ("who is Tamar in this save?") is a
    simple query we don't currently have.

  — Arnold identity: Ludmila has a crush on Arnold (id 38032). Relevant if her
    marriage trajectory becomes a planning question. Same simple-lookup gap.

  — Succession law: Still unknown from the save data alone. Affects the urgency
    of the younger sons' inheritance planning.
