Plans Archive — Playtest 2: Vratislav II Přemyslid
================================================================

This file holds plans that are no longer on the active planning table.
Each entry preserves: original plan text, original rationale, and a resolution
note. Resolution notes distinguish between "achieved as planned," "achieved
differently," "not achieved," and "superseded." The "not-as-planned" cases are
especially important — they reveal background assumptions that weren't warranted
and should inform how we reason about future plans.

Format per entry:
  [DATE FILED] [CATEGORY] [STATUS]
  ORIGINAL PLAN: ...
  ORIGINAL RATIONALE: ...
  RESOLUTION (dated when closed): ...

[1066.10.5] [FAMILY] [ACHIEVED AS PLANNED]
  ORIGINAL PLAN: Change Judita's education focus: Intrigue → Diplomacy

  ORIGINAL RATIONALE: Intrigue focus is wrong for her. Charming gives +5 Diplomacy,
  lifting effective Diplomacy to 9 while Intrigue sits at 5. Her personality
  (generous, humble, calm, charming) is made for trust and alliance, not deception.
  Free change was available.

  RESOLUTION (closed 1071.2.5): Focus is Diplomacy at 1071.2.5. Effective Diplomacy
  is 9 as projected. The plan was followed.
  Note: Judita is currently in Regensburg, not Praha. Possible betrothal or foreign
  court placement, consistent with the long-term alliance-bride trajectory we planned.


[1066.10.5] [FAMILY] [ACHIEVED AS PLANNED]
  ORIGINAL PLAN: Change Břetislav's education focus: Intrigue → Diplomacy

  ORIGINAL RATIONALE: URGENT. Effective Intrigue was -3 — honest + compassionate
  actively penalize it. He is constitutionally unsuited to scheming. Effective
  Diplomacy was 11 due to charming (+5) stacking with other ruler-virtue traits.
  Free change available.

  RESOLUTION (closed 1071.2.5): Focus is Diplomacy at 1071.2.5. Effective Diplomacy
  has grown to 15 — even stronger than projected. The Intrigue problem (was -3) is
  now 0 (recovered as the education pressure shifted). Both the mechanical and
  character outcomes exceeded expectations.


[1066.10.5] [FAMILY] [ACHIEVED AS PLANNED]
  ORIGINAL PLAN: Assign Břetislav as Vratislav's ward (Vratislav as guardian)

  ORIGINAL RATIONALE: No guardian was set. As the probable heir, Břetislav should
  be raised under Vratislav's direct eye. Paternal guardianship carries narrative
  weight (father teaching son to rule) and keeps succession preparation in-house.
  Vratislav's own moderate-high Learning is adequate for a Diplomacy focus.

  RESOLUTION (closed 1071.2.5): Confirmed. Personal relations at 1071.2.5 show
  guardian ↔ ward bond between Vratislav and Břetislav. Effective Diplomacy at 15
  suggests the education quality is good. The bond is active.
  Bonus development: Břetislav is friends with Mutina, son of Časlav Vrsovid —
  a relationship that formed during this period, possibly through guardian-connected
  court life.


[1066.10.5] [FAMILY] [NOT ACHIEVED / SUPERSEDED]
  ORIGINAL PLAN: Assign Jan of Opava as Judita's guardian

  ORIGINAL RATIONALE: Jan is Vratislav's friend — warmth already exists between
  households. His traits (just, zealous, humble, devoted) complement Judita's
  personality. His Diplomacy (8) and Learning (18) are excellent for a
  Diplomacy-focus education. Narrative richness: bishop-scholar mentoring the
  duke's eldest daughter.
  Alternative if refused: Časlav of Žatec (D=10, Diplomacy tier 4 education).

  RESOLUTION (closed 1071.2.5): No guardian-ward bond appears in Judita's personal
  relations at 1071.2.5. She has no guardian listed. She is also currently in
  Regensburg rather than Praha, which suggests she may have been sent abroad
  (betrothed or placed at a German court) before or instead of the guardianship
  being arranged.
  Key assumption violated: We assumed Jan was the natural recipient of a
  guardianship request. Unknown whether the request was made and refused, never
  made, or overtaken by Judita's departure. Časlav Vrsovid (the fallback) was
  fired from the council in this same period, which would have foreclosed that
  option too.
  Confirmed finding (post-session): The Jan guardianship was attempted and blocked
  by a game restriction. Clerical vassals (and/or realm chaplains specifically)
  cannot be assigned as guardians — the UI did not offer the option, even though
  non-clerical vassal guardianships were available. This is a mechanical constraint
  not visible from the save data alone. Narratively unintuitive: a bishop-scholar
  is exactly who you'd expect a medieval duke to entrust with his daughter's
  education, but the game disallows it.
  See ideas/ideas.txt (Guardian restriction catalog, Guardian finder) for the
  broader implication for tooling.
  What this reveals: A "find me a current guardian for this child" query would be
  useful — the plan was based on a snapshot of 1066 availability that aged quickly,
  and it needs to exclude ineligible characters (clerical, realm chaplain, etc.).


[1066.10.5] [FAMILY] [NOT ACHIEVED — OUTCOME ACCEPTABLE]
  ORIGINAL PLAN: Assign Stewardship focus for Boleslav at age 6 (due ~1069)

  ORIGINAL RATIONALE: Diligent + rowdy — hard worker with restless energy.
  Diligent naturally boosts Stewardship and Learning. Stewardship focus is the
  natural alignment.

  RESOLUTION (closed 1071.2.5): Boleslav (age 8 at assessment) has Martial focus,
  not Stewardship. The plan was not followed.
  Outcome assessment: In practice, the diligent trait provides +2 to Stewardship
  and Learning regardless of focus, so his effective Stewardship (6) and Learning (6)
  are still healthy. The mechanical gap is real but smaller than it would be for
  a character without diligent. The character outcome is different: Boleslav is
  developing as a soldier-administrator rather than a pure administrator.
  Key assumption violated: We assumed the focus would be set at 6 as planned.
  In practice, Martial focus may have been chosen for gameplay or narrative reasons
  we don't have visibility into. No guardian listed — this remains an active gap.


[1066.10.5] [COURT] [RESOLVED — SURVIVAL]
  ORIGINAL PLAN: Watch for Vladislav's illness resolution

  ORIGINAL RATIONALE: Ill at age 1. Child mortality at this age is real. His
  effective Prowess was already -4. No planning committed until he either
  recovers or does not.

  RESOLUTION (closed 1071.2.5): Vladislav survived. Health is now 4.1 (normal)
  at age 6. However, the illness appears to have left a mark: he has acquired
  the possessed_1 trait, a mental health condition with negative social effects.
  His personality is trusting + rowdy. He is friends with Boleslav.
  Status: Alive, healthy enough to plan around, but with a complicating trait.
  The "survive the illness first" gate is cleared; succession and character planning
  can now proceed. Possessed_1 should inform what role we envision for him
  (the church is a traditional placement for troubled younger sons; it may be
  more than usually appropriate here).

[1071.2.5] [FOREIGN] [ACHIEVED — AHEAD OF SCHEDULE]
  ORIGINAL PLAN: Claim title of King of Bohemia (savings goal; medium-to-long term)

  ORIGINAL RATIONALE: Reaching king tier unlocks peer-king alliances. Gold
  needed to be saved; no specific timeline given. The existing -20 disapproving
  opinion of Heinrich meant the imperial grant path was blocked; the title-
  creation path was the primary option.

  RESOLUTION (closed 1077.2.10): k_bohemia created 1072.1.12 — within 11 months
  of the plan being written. This is dramatically faster than expected. Mechanism
  likely: direct title creation (gold + prestige) rather than imperial grant,
  given the speed and the Heinrich relationship. Vratislav was simultaneously
  elevated from Duke to King and immediately used the new authority to grant
  d_meissen to Reginar two weeks later. The savings goal was apparently already
  met or the cost was lower than assumed.


[1071.2.5] [COURT] [ACHIEVED AS PLANNED]
  ORIGINAL PLAN: Grant Duchy of Meissen to Reginar

  ORIGINAL RATIONALE: Reginar (content + trusting) was the safest possible
  recipient of the d_meissen title. His positive disposition made him unlikely
  to weaponize a duchy against us. Otto von Weimar (-25) was ineligible.
  Ludwig's +20 goodwill was temporary (expiring ~1081).

  RESOLUTION (closed 1077.2.10): d_meissen granted to Reginar 1072.1.26, exactly
  two weeks after becoming King of Bohemia. Reginar's current opinion is +55
  (received_title_duchy +60, partially offset by a conversion modifier -20).
  The plan worked exactly as intended. He remains a feudal duke vassal with the
  highest positive opinion of any notable vassal.


[1071.2.5] [COURT] [ACHIEVED — MECHANISM DIFFERENT]
  ORIGINAL PLAN: Forge marriage alliances with Saxon vassal households
  (Ludwig Ludowinger's children, Benno's children, Reginar's son Liudolf)

  ORIGINAL RATIONALE: Marriage bonds create inter-household dependencies that
  make rebellion more costly. Ludwig's +20 modifier expiring ~1081 created
  timing pressure.

  RESOLUTION (closed 1077.2.10): The alliance goal was achieved at a much higher
  tier than planned — England (via Břetislav ↔ Agathe), Denmark (via Ludmila ↔

  Saxon county-level ones. Ludwig became Duke of Thuringia (d_thuringia created
  1072.3.17), which elevated him above the county tier and changed the
  calculus entirely. Whether specific Saxon-household marriages were also
  arranged is unknown; the three royal alliances dominate the diplomatic picture.
  Key assumption violated: we planned for Saxon count-level marriages; the
  actual trajectory vaulted directly to royal tier, making the Saxon marriages
  less critical as stabilizers.
