Plans — Vratislav II Přemyslid, King of Bohemia
Date: 1077.2.10  |  Playtest 2
================================================================

Living planning document. See plans/archive/plans_archive.txt for resolved
items from earlier plan sets (1066.10.5 and 1071.2.5).

Temporal categories: IMMEDIATE (next session), SHORT-TERM (1-5 years),
LONG-TERM (5-20 years), GRAND AMBITIONS (generational).

The dominant strategic fact of 1077: Bohemia is a king-tier realm with three
royal alliances and a stable (if restive) Saxon bloc. The dominant structural
problem: Seniority succession with an excommunicated man (Jaromír) second in
line. The dominant personal risk: Vratislav has consumption + health 3.5 at
age 45. The dominant narrative development: Jaromír is excommunicated, holds
a grudge against Jan, and is second in the succession line. Also ongoing and
initially overlooked: Reginar's vassal Benno von Lóningen is fighting a
claimant war to seat Otto von Weimar as Duke of Meissen (started 1073.7.25).


================================================================
I. IMMEDIATE
================================================================

[VASSAL WAR] Reginar's war in Meissen — monitor and decide on intervention
  Context: Benno von Lóningen (Count of Wolkenstein, one of Reginar's own
    Saxon counts) formed a claimant faction in July 1073 to seat Otto von
    Weimar as Duke of Meissen. The war has dragged for 3.5 years (July 1073
    → February 1077) with warscore barely in the attackers' favor (+4.846).
  Current troop balance:
    Defender (Reginar + Ludwig): ~2,607 current / ~2,760 max combined
    Attacker (Benno + Otto):       ~583 current /   ~711 max combined
    Reginar has a roughly 4-to-1 troop advantage but isn't pushing it.
  Stakes: If Otto wins, a hostile duke (declared_war -25 opinion) replaces
    Reginar (+55 opinion) in Meissen. Bohemia's most loyal Saxon vassal
    would be gone. Ludwig was called to help defend — showing the Saxon bloc
    can coordinate, but they need the king's backing to close this out.
  Options:
    A. Intervene directly with Vratislav's 4,205-troop force — decisive
       but costs a campaign season and opens Bohemia to risk
    B. Call Reginar's levies to be more aggressive with existing forces
       (in-game nudge: move armies to push sieges in Meissen)
    C. Wait and watch — the troop balance favors Reginar; patience may work,
       but the 3.5-year stalemate suggests AI Reginar won't close it alone
  Recommendation: At minimum, check in-game whether you can intervene as
    the liege without triggering complications. If the option is available
    cleanly, do it. Reginar winning cements the Saxon bloc. Reginar losing
    is the worst internal outcome possible at this stage.

[SUCCESSION] Deal with the Jaromír problem — the specific urgent priority
  Clarification: Konrád inheriting is not the crisis. Playing as Konrád is
    acceptable. The specific problem is Jaromír (age 38, EXCOMMUNICATED) being
    SECOND in the succession line. If Konrád dies before the law changes or
    Jaromír's claim is removed, an excommunicated man becomes King of Bohemia.
  The goal is "Jaromír does not inherit," not "Břetislav inherits."
  Solutions (in rough order of elegance):
    A. Change succession law to Primogeniture — removes everyone from active
       succession except Bresislav; Konrád's +55 suggests he won't fight hard
    B. Push Jaromír into the church — voluntary monastery entry removes him from
       lay succession entirely; his current marginalized state (grudge against Jan,
       excommunicated, hunter lifestyle) may make him receptive or vulnerable
    C. Disinherit or imprison Jaromír — requires cause (caught scheme, etc.);
       politically messy but possible if he acts against the realm
    D. Passive: Jaromír's health (3.9 below-avg at 38) may resolve it naturally,
       especially if the excommunication generates stress/health events
  Mechanism for law change: In CK3, succession law changes require vassal
    approval or cost prestige/influence. Konrád (+55), Reginar (+55), Ota
    (unknown), and Časlav (+30) are all in a favorable-to-moderate band.
  Priority A (law change) and Priority B (Jaromír's church path) can be
    pursued simultaneously — they are not mutually exclusive.

[SUCCESSION] Know the line and watch it
  1. Konrád (age 42, health 3.9) — acceptable as heir; not an emergency
  2. Jaromír (age 38, health 3.9, EXCOMMUNICATED) — the emergency case
  3. Ota Přemyslid (id 35734, age 32, health 4.5) — healthy; if Konrád dies
     and Jaromír is still in the line, Ota is the only buffer before Jaromír
  4. Břetislav (age 19) — would require all three above to predecease him
     under Seniority; this is why law reform matters

  Monitor: Konrád's health (3.9 below-avg); if he dies, Jaromír inherits
  unless the law has changed or Jaromír's claim is removed.

[FAMILY] Address the Bořivoj trajectory decision (~now, was ~1075)
  Who: Bořivoj (age 13, greedy + trusting + diligent + charming, Dipl focus)
  Status: Under Vratislav's guardianship with Diplomacy focus. This is a change
    from the 1071 plan (Intrigue was his focus then). Decision to make:
    Is this the final trajectory (Diplomacy → lay career, possibly in Bohemia's
    court or minor holding)? Or is the church path still viable?
  Current lean: With diligent + charming and a Diplomacy focus, he is developing
    as a plausible courtier or administrator. The greedy+trusting profile is
    less dangerous in a non-ruling role. The church path for greedy characters
    creates its own scandal risks. Lay courtier is probably the right call.
  Action: Commit to the lay path. Identify a marriage target for ~1081 (age 17).

[HEALTH] Monitor Vratislav's consumption
  Context: Consumption (tuberculosis/wasting) is a progressive CK3 health trait.
    Combined with health 3.5 (below average), the mortality risk over the next
    decade is real. There is no guaranteed way to remove this trait — it may
    require a specific health event chain or a court physician event.
  Dimi (id 40499) is Vratislav's court physician. His competence affects
    health event outcomes. Worth noting as a key position.
  Action: Do not trigger unnecessary health-damaging events (wars, stress,
    character interactions that generate stress traits).

II. SHORT-TERM (~1077–1082)
================================================================

[FOREIGN] Hungary alliance — still the key gap
  Context: Three royal alliances now (England, Denmark, Poland), but no
    Hungarian bond. Géza (King of Hungary, 4,275 troops, nearby, Catholic)
    is the obvious missing partner. Hungary is the most important regional
    power with whom we have no relationship.
  Mechanism: A marriage between a younger Přemyslid and a Hungarian noble.
    Boleslav (age 14, opens ~1079) is the most available piece. Alternatively,
    a diplomatic approach through Bretislav's English alliance (William has
    continental reach). Check whether Géza has unmarried children.

[FOREIGN] The Papal problem — reclassify as URGENT
  Context: Anselmo still -30. Now we also have Jaromír excommunicated. An
    excommunicated Přemyslid is second in the succession line. If Konrád dies
    and the succession law hasn't changed, an excommunicated man becomes King
    of Bohemia — which may trigger papal intervention.
  Jaromír dimension: His excommunication may be separable from Vratislav's
    impious_opinion. The -30 on Vratislav predates Jaromír's excommunication.
    But having both the king and his second-in-line at odds with Rome is a
    serious vulnerability.
  Immediate action: Find out what drives the -30. Is it a lifestyle trait?
    A sin? A specific decision?

[FAMILY] Vladislav's trajectory — reassess given Polish guardianship
  Who: Vladislav (age 12, Kraków, guardian = King Bolesław III)
  Context: He has Martial focus under Poland's king. He's gained "ambitious"
    as a trait since 1071 — positive for lay career, complicated for a
    younger son. The possessed_1 trait persists; no resolution event fired.
  The church path is now less appropriate (ambitious + martial). The more
    likely trajectory is a military/court career in Bohemia or Poland.
  Question: Will he return to Praha at the end of his guardianship? Or will
    Bolesław keep him as a courtier/commander? This matters for succession
    (he is 7th in line, behind many Přemyslids, but still ahead of Slavibor
    and Mikul).

[FAMILY] Boleslav's guardian quality — assess Petr
  Who: Boleslav (age 14, Brno, guardian Petr id 55287)
  Context: Petr is unknown to us. We know Boleslav is at Brno (Konrád's court)
    with Martial focus and rivals with Adelheid (id 39569), a 12-year-old
    Saxon girl from the Stade area. The rivalry is unexplained.
  Action: Profile Petr. If his Martial skills are poor, consider a guardian
    change before Boleslav's education window closes.
  Note on Adelheid: Her dynasty head is Lothar-Udo (id 31612). The rivalry
    between Boleslav (in Brno) and Adelheid (at Stade) suggests they were at
    the same court at some point. Context unknown but worth monitoring — she
    could become a marriage candidate or political complication.

[FAMILY] Bretislav's marriage — confirm Agathe
  Context: English alliance runs through "Břetislav ↔ Agathe" but Agathe does
    not appear in the living characters search. She may be at the English court
    (not in Bohemia), encoded differently, or deceased.
  Action: Confirm in-game whether Agathe is alive and where she is. If they
    are betrothed rather than married, this is the window to complete the marriage
    (Bretislav is 19). If she is deceased, the English alliance may be at risk
    of dissolving.

[FAMILY] Slavibor — hunchbacked
  Who: Slavibor (id 16820219, born 1073.2.6, age 4)
  Context: Has the "hunchbacked" congenital physical trait. This is a negative

  Implication: Future children may also be at risk. Worth noting when planning

[DOMAIN] Čáslav development — continue investment

  Action: Shift the Čáslav building queue to the special economic building.

III. LONG-TERM (~1082–1095)
================================================================

[SUCCESSION] Bretislav's reign preparation — contingent on succession reform
  If the succession law is changed to Primogeniture, Bretislav (effective Dipl 20,
  tier 3 education, English alliance) is well-positioned to inherit. His trait
  profile (compassionate, honest, generous) is exactly right for a trust-based
  reign. He needs:
    — A domain (ideally a county before inheriting the duchy/kingdom)
    — Confirmed marriage (Agathe situation resolved)
    — No hooks on Bretislav from hostile parties (Šwiétosława's intrigue layer
      remains a concern; Záviš at Intrigue 15 is still present)
  If the succession law is NOT changed, Bretislav's long-term trajectory must
  account for the possibility that he inherits only after Konrád and Ota (and
  Ota's sons) all die — which could be 30-40 years from now.

[SUCCESSION] Managing the Přemyslid seniority line
  Even after law reform, Konrád's and Ota's claim does not disappear — it
  becomes inactive (a claim rather than the succession). They could press claims
  through wars. Managing their loyalty is therefore a permanent constraint.
  Key: Konrád (+55 opinion) is already well-managed. Ota's opinion is unclear
  but he was Ludmila's guardian (presumably well-treated). Their children's
  claims extend the problem into the next generation.

[DYNASTIC] The Jaromír problem — resolution required
  Jaromír is excommunicated, holds a Grudge against Jan, is Vratislav's rival,
  and is second in the Bohemian succession line. The combination is intolerable.
  Options:
    A. Pressure Jan to dismiss him from court service (the "task" assignment
       suggests Jan still has some obligation to or over him)
    B. Find grounds for imprisonment (if he schemes against Vratislav, a caught-
       scheme would allow imprisonment)
    C. Encourage him to enter a monastery (voluntary departure from lay life)
    D. Do nothing and rely on natural mortality (health 3.9, age 38 — plausible
       but not reliable)
  The excommunication itself may resolve if Jaromír atones (does penance events).
  His becoming a monk or friar would remove him from lay succession entirely.

[DYNASTIC] Succession law reform — the generational question
  If Primogeniture is achieved in the next 5 years, the long-term question
  becomes: hold it through Bratislav's reign. Primogeniture requires all
  major vassals to accept it. The Saxon bloc (Reginar, Ludwig), despite current
  positive opinions, will have their own succession interests. Reginar's +55
  is the best possible foundation for getting his buy-in. Ludwig's opinion is
  not visible in notable opinions, suggesting it is moderate — needs monitoring.

[DYNASTIC] The Polish Connection — reassess under Bolesław III
  Vladislav is being raised by Bolesław III (King of Poland). This is a
  significant diplomatic bond. A Bolesław-friendly Přemyslid at Bratislav's
  future court (whether as advisor, marshal, or courtier) extends the
  Czech-Polish axis into the next generation. If Vladislav's possessed_1
  trait generates negative event chains, the Polish connection may sour.


================================================================
IV. GRAND AMBITIONS
================================================================

[DYNASTIC] Přemyslid Bohemia: Stability Through Character
  Unchanged. The goal is to hand Bratislav a realm in better shape than it
  began. The Saxon integration is proceeding (Reginar +55, Ludwig elevated).
  The succession law is the remaining structural barrier. Bratislav's trait
  profile remains excellent. The counterweights (Záviš, Jaromír, Bořivoj's
  greedy+trusting) are less threatening now that Bořivoj is on a Diplomacy
  path under Vratislav's direct guardianship.

[DYNASTIC] The Royal Project: Hold What Was Built
  Kingdom of Bohemia exists. Three royal alliances exist. The next question is
  whether these achievements survive Vratislav's death intact. If he dies under
  Seniority succession, Konrád becomes King — and Konrád, while loyal, is not
  the ruler we've been building toward. All the alliance bonds (via Bratislav,
  Ludmila) may weaken under a different king. The urgency of succession reform
  is thus not just about Bratislav's inheritance — it is about preserving the
  diplomatic architecture that took 11 years to build.
