Four years and four months since the opening position. The two most urgent
recommendations from the prior assessment have been executed: the Jaromír
problem is resolved, and Poland is now an ally. On top of that, Vratislav has
achieved something the opening assessment did not contemplate as near-term —
elevation to King of Bohemia. The position is materially stronger on almost
every axis. But the expansion that came with the elevation has introduced a
new class of problem: restive German vassals, a live claimant faction, and
visible papal displeasure.


I. WHAT CHANGED: THE HEADLINE MOVES
-------------------------------------

King of Bohemia:
  Vratislav is no longer Duke — he holds the kingdom title. This is the
  defining event of the period and a major acceleration of the game's central
  ambition. The elevation brought new direct vassals (German counts in
  Thuringia and Saxony) and substantially expanded the realm's levy base, but
  also imported a set of disloyal lords who did not choose their new suzerain.

Jaromír removed from Opava:
  The opening assessment identified Jaromír Přemyslid — mutual rival,
  theocracy vassal, border county holder — as the most damaging relationship
  in the realm. He is now unlanded and in another court, stripped of his power
  base. His friend Jan (previously an unlanded courtier in Vratislav's own
  court, and a mutual friend since 1066) now holds the Count of Opava title as
  a theocracy vassal. The rivalry with Jaromír technically persists but is
  operationally neutered — a rival without land or levy is an irritant, not a
  threat. This was cleanly executed.

Poland alliance secured:
  The opening assessment named Poland as the most important external
  relationship to resolve. It is now resolved: Bolesław III Piast (King of
  Poland, 2,981 troops) is an ally via Šwiétosława's mediation. The +10
  spouse_mediation_opinion confirms the mechanism. Poland and Bohemia are now
  on the same side of any potential northern conflict — a significant
  stabiliser on the left flank.


II. THE REALM: INTERNAL STRUCTURE AND CURRENT RISKS
----------------------------------------------------

Domain and Bohemian core:
  Vratislav's personal domain is unchanged at seven holdings (Praha 12 dev,
  six others at 8 dev each), same terrain mix, same 100% control, same 60
  total development. No domain expansion occurred; the growth came entirely
  from new vassals. Development investment at Čáslav (the special economic
  site) should still be the priority here — four years on without visible
  progress is a missed compounding opportunity.

The German counties:
  Four new direct feudal/theocracy vassals appear that were not present in
  1066 — all from central German territory to the northwest:
    Benno von Lóningen, Count of Wolkenstein (1,049 troops, hills×2)
    Ludwig Ludowinger, Count of Schmalkalden (1,006 troops, plains×2
      + mountains×1, 1 holding at low control)
    Otto von Weimar, Count of Meissen (857 troops, forest×2, 1 low control)
    Eberhard, Count of Naumburg (526 troops, theocracy, plains×1, 1 low ctrl)

  Three of the four have at least one holding at low control — characteristic
  of recently subjugated territory that hasn't been fully pacified. Otto von
  Weimar holds a "declared_war -25" opinion malus against Vratislav, confirming
  that he was brought in through conquest, not diplomacy. Oda (almost certainly
  a relative of Otto) has a "declared_war_on_son_daughter -15" malus. These
  are new vassals who remember being on the other side.

The claimant faction:
  Ludwig Ludowinger and Otto von Weimar — two of the four new German counts —
  have formed a claimant_faction with 2 members and a combined force of ~1,906
  troops. The faction's specific demand is not yet resolved in the evaluator,
  but a claimant faction from newly conquered German lords almost certainly
  presses either a claim to Bohemia itself or to the territory they lost.
  Militarily this is manageable: 1,906 versus a realm total of 4,802 plus the
  Polish alliance (2,981) is not a close fight. Politically, the correct
  response is to either buy them off (give Ludwig or Otto a title or position),
  address the underlying claim via marriage, or crush the faction quickly before
  it attracts additional members. Leaving it to fester risks it growing.

The Moravian Přemyslids:
  Konrád Přemyslid (Count of Brno) has grown slightly to 1,442 troops with
  a new +30 courtly_title_creation opinion — Vratislav gave him a court
  position, an obvious and sensible opinion management tool. Similarly Ota
  Přemyslid (Count of Olomouc) has +30 from the same. Both Přemyslid cousins
  are now actively cultivated rather than passively tolerated. This is exactly
  the right approach given their structural weight in the realm.

Česlav Vrsovid:
  The Vrsovid count of Žatec, flagged in the opening assessment as a potential
  faction nucleus, now sits at +80 opinion via two successful sway schemes and
  a court title. He has been thoroughly co-opted. Whether this represents
  genuine loyalty or mere pacification will matter at succession, but for now
  he is not a problem.

House hooks:
  Hooks held have expanded from three children (Boleslav, Bořivoj, Vladislav)
  to six (adding Judita, Albrecht, Marie). Marie is listed as a new child — the
  family now has seven children. This is succession depth.


III. MILITARY POSTURE
----------------------

Realm max strength has grown from 3,292 to 4,802 — a 46% increase in four
years. The levy base expanded from 2,467 to 3,776 (+53%), almost entirely
from the new German vassal levies. MaA cap grew marginally from 825 to 1,026.
Combat power: 91,580 → 106,140 (+16%).

The MaA composition is unchanged (zbrojnosh 500, light horsemen 200, pikemen
100, mangonel 20), which is a mild concern — the realm is bigger now and the
absolute MaA count has not scaled proportionally. Expanding the zbrojnosh
regiment in particular would compound Bohemia's existing defensive advantage.

Knights improved: seven knights now (up from five), with three at prowess 10
(Petr, Kunrát, Lanź) — Lanź's improvement from prowess 9 to 10 is notable.
The bench is meaningfully deeper than in 1066.

No armies are currently raised; no wars are active. The peace window is an
asset — it allows levy recovery and faction management without military
distraction.


IV. IMPERIAL AND RELIGIOUS SITUATION
--------------------------------------

The Investiture prediction:
  The opening assessment noted that Heinrich's coming conflict with the Saxons
  and the Investiture Controversy would force Bohemia to choose sides. It
  appears that choice has been made. Pope Anselmo (the game's Papal character)
  holds a -30 impious_opinion against Vratislav — a significant penalty that
  suggests Vratislav has aligned with the Emperor against the Church, consistent
  with the historical record (Heinrich IV and Vratislav were allied in the
  Investiture period, and Vratislav's elevation to King was Heinrich's reward).

  The "Henry -50 insult_opinion" from Vratislav is harder to read without more
  context. If Henry is Emperor Heinrich, this is a significant deterioration in
  the imperial relationship — a formal insult is a diplomatic escalation, not
  just a passive opinion drift. If Henry is someone else (a Saxon prince, a
  deposed claimant), it may be a side effect of the German expansion. Either
  way it warrants attention: Vratislav's position in the HRE depends on
  Heinrich's goodwill, and an open insult event is not background noise.

Catholic fervor: 50.0 → 41.8.
  Catholic fervor has declined by 8 points while pagan fervor has risen
  noticeably: slavic pagan 50.0 → 58.1, baltic pagan 50.0 → 59.9, norse pagan
  50.0 → 64.0, finnish pagan 50.0 → 54.7, tengri pagan 50.0 → 52.7. This is
  a systemic shift in the religious landscape — paganism is growing more
  fervent while Catholicism weakens. High fervor paganism is more resistant to
  conversion and more likely to launch holy wars. This accelerates the timeline
  on the northern pagan question.

Siemomysl of Pomerania:
  This is the most alarming single change in the external picture. Pomerania
  has grown from 2,735 troops in 1066 to 4,771 in 1070 — a 74% increase in
  four years. Slavic pagan fervor is up 8 points. The prior assessment
  described the northern pagan belt as a medium-term threat; it is now a more
  immediate one. Pomerania alone (4,771 troops) now exceeds Bohemia's entire
  realm strength before the German expansion, and is within striking range of
  Bohemia's northwestern border. This should be the primary external
  surveillance focus going forward.


V. THREATS, OPPORTUNITIES, AND PRIORITIES
------------------------------------------

Immediate (resolve in this season):
  1. The claimant faction — Ludwig and Otto will not disband on their own.
     Options: grant one of them a richer title or county to detach their
     loyalty, force-imprison the faction leader, or let it ripen into war and
     crush it (clean but expensive). Given their weak combined strength, the
     military option is actually viable and would remove the problem permanently.
     The key risk is that fighting vassals damages realm opinion broadly.

  2. The Papal relationship — -30 impious is not yet catastrophic but it
     compounds with everything else. The Pope controls the Investiture dynamic
     and can legitimize or delegitimize claims against Bohemia. A modest
     pilgrimage or charity action to recover some Papal goodwill costs little
     and insures against excommunication, which at -30 is not yet threatened
     but is worth not approaching.

  3. Develop Čáslav — the special economic building site has been idle for
     four years. Development compounds; the cost of the delay grows each year.

Near-term (next year or two):
  4. Pacify the German counties — Meissen and Schmalkalden both have low-
     control holdings. Raising control here reduces the faction risk and
     normalises the new vassals. A steward mission or direct investment is
     appropriate.

  5. Expand MaA — the zbrojnosh regiment in particular should be increased
     now that the MaA cap has grown to 1,026. More zbrojnosh means more
     defensive leverage on home terrain, which becomes valuable quickly if
     Pomerania presses northward or a German power contests the new counties.

  6. Watch Pomerania — Siemomysl's 74% growth in four years is the most
     important trend in the regional picture. If this rate continues, Pomerania
     will be at ~8,000 troops by 1075. At that point a holy war against Bohemia
     or Poland becomes conceivable. Consider whether a pre-emptive holy war is
     available and whether the timing is right — the Polish alliance makes a
     joint campaign against Pomeranian paganism feasible.

  7. Secure a Hungarian alliance — this was a priority in the opening assessment
     and remains unresolved. The Polish alliance covers the northern flank;
     Hungary on the southern flank completes the defensive perimeter. An Árpád
     marriage for one of the seven children is the mechanism.

Bottom line:
  The position is significantly stronger than four years ago. King title secured,
  rival neutralised, Poland allied, realm levy nearly doubled. The two problems
  carried forward from 1066 — Jaromír and Poland — are closed. The new problems
  are the faction and the papal friction, both byproducts of the German expansion
  and Investiture alignment. Neither is existential. The main strategic concern
  going forward is Pomerania, whose rapid growth makes the northern pagan
  question time-sensitive in a way it was not at game start. The Polish alliance
  makes joint action there feasible; the question is whether to move first or
  wait for a defensive trigger.
